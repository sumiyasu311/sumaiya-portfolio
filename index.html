About Me

I believe good pharmacy is equal parts scientific rigour, clear communication, and genuine care for people.

I am a pharmacy student at Manarat International University with a growing focus on digital health, pharmacovigilance, and cheminformatics. My academic work explores how mobile health tools, safety reporting systems, and computational methods can support safer, more patient-centred outcomes.

I enjoy turning complex scientific information into clear, useful communication — from structured research writing to thoughtful visual design. I am looking for opportunities where I can apply analytical thinking, laboratory discipline, and digital tools to real-world healthcare challenges.
