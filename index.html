About Me

A pharmacist in the making,
curious about the digital side of care.

I am a pharmacy student at Manarat International University, Dhaka, with a strong interest in the intersection of clinical pharmacy and digital technology. My academic work explores how open-source computational tools and mobile health applications can strengthen pharmacy education, drug-safety surveillance, and patient care.

Alongside my studies, I continue to develop my skills in Microsoft Office, laboratory techniques, and creative design. I believe a modern pharmacist should be equally comfortable at the bench, in the clinic, and in the digital space.

This portfolio brings together my academic projects, skills, and interests. It reflects how I am preparing myself for a profession where scientific knowledge and digital literacy increasingly go hand in hand.
