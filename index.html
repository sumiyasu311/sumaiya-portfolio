Who I am

About Me

I believe the future of pharmacy is equal parts science and data — and that good communication is what turns both into better care.

I'm a pharmacy student at Manarat International University with a growing focus on digital health, cheminformatics, and medication safety. My academic work explores how mobile health tools, pharmacovigilance systems, and computational methods can support safer, more patient-centred outcomes.

Beyond the classroom, I enjoy translating complex scientific ideas into clear, useful communication — from well-structured reports to thoughtful visual design. I care about accuracy, curiosity, and making information accessible to the people who need it.

Better health begins with better information — and better information begins with careful, curious people.
